the author asked, so his four plugins are gone - and the framework's copy of his work with them

OpticalCamo, TimeWeatherControl, ammo_capacity and NPCSpawner were
re-implementations of IHateHUDClutter's plugins, two of them credited by name in
the README. On 2026-09-20 he asked that his work be removed from the repository
as well as from the release. This is that removal, and with it the parts of the
framework that were his rather than ours:

- the four plugin folders go, and with them their build entries in
  build_msvc.ps1, their Makefile rules, their names in the packaging whitelist,
  their rows in the README's feature table and the two credit lines - replaced
  by one line saying the work was removed at the author's request and pointing
  at his own releases - and the OpticalCamo block in the reference
  scripthook.ini, which now says the same thing;
- the shipped set is six plugins: skipintro, spawner, firstperson, fov_changer,
  cnchat, micfix. A -Beta / -Release build deploys six, and a package carries
  six;
- scripthook_npc.c no longer carries the four per-archetype id tables, the
  44 entry blacklist or the single Unidad id. They had been recovered from his
  plugin and were the only place in the framework that held his data. What is
  left is the engine's own kind, which is what the rest of the catalogue was
  always grouped by - so an archetype whose kind does not name its faction now
  reads as the group its kind names. The five formations are re-derived the
  same way: the shapes stay, the spacing and the jitter are ours (one
  xorshift32 step per point, each point kept inside its own slice);
- the docs in scripthook.h describe the grouping that way instead of naming his
  plugin, and the two comments that pointed at removed code are reworded.
  scripthook_ammocap.c keeps its hook and its "AmmoCapacity.asi is loaded"
  guard - a file name is not his work, and the guard protects a player who runs
  his plugin and this framework together - but its header no longer calls
  itself his plugin's replacement.

The functionality is not lost: the framework still exports what all four
features need (ShSetTime*, ShSetWeather*, ShSetAmmoScale, ShSetVisibility, the
NPC spawn and formation calls), so any of them can come back as an independent
plugin written from the game side. The README says where to get his, which are
updated for TU25 and are his to distribute.

Measured: build clean (no errors, no warnings) with the six plugin set, and
nothing of his is left on the development machine either - the copies the build
had moved into plugins_off\ and the build's own backup folders are gone.
